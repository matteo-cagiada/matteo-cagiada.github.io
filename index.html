Matteo Cagiada

Novo Nordisk Foundation Postdoctoral Fellow · University of Oxford & University of Copenhagen

I study how proteins move. I use AI models, from protein language models to generative and diffusion models, to predict conformational ensembles and side-chain dynamics, and apply them to antibodies, TCRs, receptors and transporters to improve how new therapeutics are designed.

With Charlotte Deane (OPIG, Statistics) and Philip Biggin (Biochemistry) in Oxford. Before that, a PhD and postdoc with Kresten Lindorff-Larsen at the Linderstrøm-Lang Centre in Copenhagen, on the effects of missense variants on protein stability and function, and a visit to Sergey Ovchinnikov's lab at Harvard.
